CRICKET UNIVERSE CHANGES
Cricket Universe performs its own team-colour material treatment, removes the
source jersey branding at runtime, retargets animation clips, controls match
cameras, positions the field, drives the ball trajectory and connects all
visuals to the authoritative Supabase match state.

The CC-BY asset credits above must remain with distributed versions of the
game.
