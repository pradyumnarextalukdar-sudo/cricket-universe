CRICKET UNIVERSE V3 — REAL RIGGED MODELS (PHASE 1)

This build discards the old capsule-player renderer for the live match.

Implemented:
- Real skinned humanoid GLB characters.
- Compatible skeletal animation library loaded with Three.js GLTFLoader.
- Skeleton-safe cloning for multiple on-field players.
- Real sprint/jog/idle animation states.
- Original cricket bowling pose layered on the humanoid armature.
- Original batting stroke pose layered on the humanoid armature.
- Cricket bat, helmet, pads and wicketkeeper gloves attached to rig bones.
- Bowler run-up.
- Batter shot and follow-through.
- Running between wickets.
- Fielder chase animation.
- Wicketkeeper movement.
- Moving bails on wickets.
- PBR grass using CC0 ambientCG maps.
- Improved pitch, crease, boundary, stadium bowl, crowd and floodlights.
- ACES tone mapping, soft shadows and improved lighting.
- Broadcast bowler-end camera plus field/boundary/wicket camera changes.
- Mobile fallback if remote rigged assets cannot load.

No Supabase SQL changes are required.

Important:
This is the first real-rigged-model graphics build. The CC0 animation library
contains general humanoid locomotion rather than motion-captured cricket
deliveries. Cricket-specific bowling/batting actions are therefore authored
procedurally on the compatible rig in this phase. Dedicated high-end cricket
motion clips are the next asset milestone.
